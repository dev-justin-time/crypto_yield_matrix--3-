calculation basis

Yield is computed as annualized total return: (ending NAV + distributions − starting NAV) ÷ starting NAV, annualized to 365 days. The enriched dataset combines staking, lending-market equivalents, protocol rewards, market, liquidity, risk, and model features.

sorting rules

Within each quarter, assets are stacked in descending annualized yield. Equal values are ordered by current market cap. Aggregate columns use the supplied prior and current 12-month mean yields and show the supplied change in percentage points.

asterisk meanings

An asterisk marks an annualized or newly listed category. These values may come from monthly snapshots, inflation pools, lending-market equivalents, or other comparable reward sources; hover a colored block for the asset-specific basis.
