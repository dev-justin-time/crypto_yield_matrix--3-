key data metrics — aggregate across all tracked assets
calculation basis
Yield is computed as annualized total return: (ending NAV + distributions − starting NAV) ÷ starting NAV, annualized to 365 days. Sources: on-chain staking data, exchange lending rates, protocol revenue distributions, and validator reward APIs.
sorting rules
Within each quarter, assets are stacked in descending order of annualized yield. Ties broken by market-cap weight. Aggregate columns show arithmetic mean of quarterly yields across the 12-month window.
source coverage
Source snapshot means this asset has a matching one-row market table under csv/. Canonical yield only means the catalog preserves the canonical matrix row and transparent derived features, but no supplied market snapshot was available. Missing values are intentionally blank.
asterisk meanings
* = newly listed or annualized category. Assets marked with an asterisk have yields annualized from monthly snapshots or lending-market equivalents due to limited on-chain staking infrastructure. Hover any block to see asset-specific notes.
